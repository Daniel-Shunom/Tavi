# Television asset attribution

Television 01 by Gabriel Radić, downloaded from Poly Haven:
https://polyhaven.com/a/Television_01

License: CC0 1.0 Universal. The model and its textures are redistributed under the source asset's CC0 dedication.

The landing hero renders the glTF model in Three.js. Its original curved glass triangles carry a live canvas texture containing the fictional stream, animated Rive character, static, signal glitches, and phosphor effects. The housing occludes the screen naturally. `television-front.png` is a front-facing render of the same asset and is used only as a fallback when WebGL is unavailable.
