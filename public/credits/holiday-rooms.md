# Holiday room models

The 3D models in Tavi's holiday rooms in Studio come from three free packs. All three are
released under CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain): https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Credit is not required by the licence; it is given here with thanks. Only the models a room uses
are bundled, unchanged, under `rooms/`, each folder with the pack's own licence file.

## KayKit: Halloween Bits 1.0

By Kay Lousberg (https://www.kaylousberg.com).
Source: https://kaylousberg.itch.io/halloween-bits and
https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Halloween-Bits-1.0
Used in the Halloween room: pumpkins, jack-o'-lanterns, candles, lanterns, skulls, the coffin
and the bone. Files: `rooms/kaykit-halloween/`.

## KayKit: Furniture Bits 1.0

By Kay Lousberg (https://www.kaylousberg.com).
Source: https://kaylousberg.itch.io/furniture-bits and
https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Furniture-Bits-1.0
Used in both rooms: rugs, the armchair and couch, tables, the cabinet, shelf, picture frames and
the standing lamp. Files: `rooms/kaykit-furniture/`.

## Kenney: Holiday Kit 2.0

By Kenney (https://www.kenney.nl).
Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/holiday-kit
Used in the Christmas room: the tree, presents, string lights, wreath, stockings, snowflakes,
nutcracker, gingerbread man, lantern, reindeer, candy cane and toy train.
Files: `rooms/kenney-holiday/`.

Downloaded November 24, 2026. The rooms are arranged in
`src/studio-next/holiday/room3d/layouts.ts`.
