## Character variants (derivatives)

Tavi's character variants are adaptations of the same CC BY 4.0 artwork (Lyla v2, the remix in
`art/mascot-remix/`). At runtime Tavi changes its colours (skin tones, eyes, hair), its proportions
(build, face shape), and hides or recolours built-in parts. It also draws original Tavi hairstyles,
head coverings and features (`src/renderer/characters/variants/overlay/`) that follow the
character's bones. Changes were made to the original. Every variant is a derivative of
"Mascot / Avatar Customization" by alisketch and keeps this attribution.
