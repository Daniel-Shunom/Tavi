## What CC BY 4.0 asks of Tavi

- **Allowed:** sharing and adapting the art, including commercially (the app, Store listing, ads, emotes).
- **Credit every time it is published:** creator, title, a source link, a licence link, and a note that it was changed. A credit in the listing or description is enough when the medium has no room (emotes, thumbnails).
- **No implied endorsement:** do not suggest alisketch endorses Tavi.
- **No extra restrictions:** do not add terms or DRM that stop others from using the original art as the licence allows.
- **Not exclusive:** the character design stays alisketch's. Anyone may use the same art, so "Tavi" (the name, wordmark and app) is distinctively yours, but the character's look is not. A wholly original character would be needed for exclusive brand ownership.
- The licence covers copyright only. Check the Rive marketplace listing for any extra terms. This is a summary, not legal advice.

## Where credit is not needed

- **Images:** no on-image credit is needed when the listing that shows them carries the block above.
- **Fonts:** DM Sans and Manrope (SIL OFL) and Georgia do not require credit in images.
- **Sound effects:** synthesized for the project (`video/capture/make-sfx.py`).
- **Landing assets:** the Poly Haven models are CC0. They are listed above only because the landing screenshot shows them. The APOD clip (CC BY 3.0) plays on the landing TV; keep its line if that screenshot is published.
